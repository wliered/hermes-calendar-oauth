Hermes Calendar

A personal calendar and email automation application for the Hailey household, Austin, TX.

About this app

Hermes Calendar is a private, self-hosted personal assistant application. It automates scheduling, email, and document tasks for its owner, Wilson Hailey, using Google services the owner has explicitly authorized.

Hermes Calendar is used exclusively by its owner. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

Features

  • Calendar automation: Creates and updates Google Calendar events, and syncs the family calendar to a private local calendar server so it stays available at home.
  • Email assistance: Reads, sends, and organizes Gmail messages on the owner's behalf.
  • File and document management: Manages Google Drive files, and reads and creates Google Sheets and Docs.
  • Contacts reference: Reads Google Contacts to support the owner's workflows.

Data access

To provide the features above, Hermes Calendar requests access to the following data from the owner's own Google account. This access is used only to perform tasks the owner initiates, and never for advertising, selling data, or any third-party purpose.

Google dataWhy the app needs it
Google CalendarCreate and update events; sync the family calendar to the owner's private server.
GmailRead, send, and manage email on the owner's behalf.
Google DriveRead and upload files when the owner asks.
Google ContactsRead contact information for reference.
Google Sheets and DocsRead and create spreadsheets and documents.

Access can be reviewed or revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page. Revoking access immediately stops all data access.
