Hermes Calendar

Hermes Calendar is a personal calendar and email automation application. It helps its owner, Wilson Hailey, manage his own Google data — Google Calendar events, Gmail, Google Drive files, Contacts, Sheets, and Docs — from privately owned home servers in Austin, TX.

Purpose

The purpose of Hermes Calendar is to automate personal scheduling and email tasks for its owner. It creates and syncs calendar events, reads and sends email, and organizes documents, all at the owner's request.

Hermes Calendar performs tasks such as:

  • Creating and updating Google Calendar events, and syncing the family calendar to a private local calendar server
  • Reading, sending, and organizing Gmail messages
  • Managing Google Drive files, Sheets, and Docs
  • Accessing Google Contacts for reference

The application is used exclusively by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no user data is shared, sold, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page.
